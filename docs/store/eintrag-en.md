# Play-Store-Eintrag auf Englisch – fertige Texte

Die englische Übersetzung des Eintrags. In der Play Console unter
**Wachstum → Store-Präsenz → Haupt-Store-Eintrag → Übersetzungen verwalten →
Übersetzungen hinzufügen → Englisch (Vereinigte Staaten) – en-US** anlegen,
dann die Felder unten einfügen. Wer auch Großbritannien eigens bedienen
will, legt **en-GB** mit denselben Texten an; Google zeigt sonst en-US.

Die Texte sind für englische Spieler geschrieben, nicht Satz für Satz
übersetzt. Die Bilder liegen in [`en/`](en/) und werden mit
`npm run build && node scripts/build-store-assets.mjs en` neu erzeugt.

**Kein Markenname im Eintrag.** In der App heißt Kniffel auf Englisch
„Yahtzee-style Dice“ (so entschieden am 06.11.2026). Im Store-Text steht
es trotzdem als „dice game“: Googles Richtlinie für Store-Texte verbietet
fremde Marken ohne Erlaubnis, und Yahtzee gehört Hasbro.

Alles Übrige (Kategorie, Kontakt, Datenschutz-Adresse, Fragebögen) gilt
für alle Sprachen gleich und steht in [`eintrag.md`](eintrag.md).

## Vollständige Beschreibung (max. 4000 Zeichen)

```
Just one more round.

TE-Mini Games is a collection of short games for the minutes in between: at
the bus stop, on a break, for two at the kitchen table or for six in the
living room. No round takes more than a few minutes, and you can stop
whenever you like.

SKILL AND REACTION
• The Perfect Second – stop the clock on the second, to the hundredth.
  500 levels through five worlds, from the jungle to the ice palace.
• Bee Flow – send out colour blocks and let the bees clear a picture pixel
  by pixel. Five slots, then the colony is stuck.
• Squishy Dumplings – three dumplings in a row, against the clock.
• Trapbound – a platformer where the floor lies.
• Emberwake – find light in a dark world before your ember goes out.

BRAIN AND MEMORY
• Sudoku – 150 puzzles in three tiers. Every tier is its own path.
• What's missing? – remember what was there a moment ago.
• Remember the Order – the sequence gets longer every time.
• Mental Maths – quick sums against the clock.

DICE, CARDS AND BOARD
• Dice game – thirteen boxes, three rolls, a bonus from 63. Every further
  five of a kind is worth 100 extra points.
• Texas Hold'em – a tournament against the computer or online against
  others.
• Mau-Mau and Last Card – two ways to play, every house rule switchable.
• Schützenopoly – a board game around German marksmen's festivals, with 22
  real festivals from all over Germany.

FOR GROUPS – ONE PHONE IS ENOUGH
• Find the Imposter, Who am I?, Categories, Charades, Word Bomb, Who would
  rather?, Marksmen Round – the phone goes once round the table.

ONLINE AGAINST EACH OTHER
The dice game, Texas Hold'em, Mau-Mau, Last Card, Schützenopoly, Marksmen
Round, Find the Imposter, Who am I? and Categories can also be played
online: open a table, share the code, play.

IN ENGLISH AND GERMAN
The app speaks both languages – with its own English words and questions
for the guessing games, not translated German ones.

NO SIGNAL NEEDED
Playing, points, XP, badges and group mode all work without internet.
You only go online when you want to.

ADS ONLY IF YOU WANT THEM
Ads only come as an optional video: watch one if you like and get a little
more time, a second try or a special item in return. Points can't be bought
this way. No in-game purchases, no tracking, no analytics. An account is
optional and only needed for online games and the leaderboard; without one,
everything stays on your device. You can delete your account in the app
itself.

Poker is played with play money. There is nothing to buy and nothing to
win.
```
