> A highly detailed, hand-painted vertical 9:16 mobile game level map, directly inspired by the style and winding stone path layout. The map illustrates a complete, seamless time-traveling journey for a dinosaur game, expanding from Level 1 to 500.
>
> The map is segmented into five distinct, visually transitioning biomes, each comprising 100 levels. The bottom-most zone (1-100) features a lush, dense jungle with a Triceratops, Stegosaurus, small Raptors, and Pterosaur.
>
> The winding stone path, marked with circular stone slab level numbers, ascends through the following zones:
>
> * Zone 2 (Level 101-200): The lush jungle transitions into a dry, rocky, volcanic landscape with smoky air and small active volcanoes in the distance. Includes a Pachycephalosaurus and a Carnotaurus.
> * Zone 3 (Level 201-300): The volcanic area gives way to a harsh, arid rock desert and canyonlands. The path is carved through deep rock with larger theropods like an Allosaurus and an Ankylosaurus.
> * Zone 4 (Level 301-400): The rock desert transitions dramatically into a deep Ice Age landscape with snow, glaciers, and frozen waterfalls, featuring Woolly Mammoths and Saber-toothed Cats.
> * Zone 5 (Level 401-500): The path ascends to the final, highest, fully glaciated peak with extreme frost, ending at an ancient ice-carved structure marking Level 500.
>
> The overall illustrative, painterly style is perfectly consistent throughout the entire map, optimized for a clean, intuitive vertical mobile layout.
